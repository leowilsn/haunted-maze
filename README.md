# Haunted Maze

A spooky top-down maze: sneak past ghosts, bats and spiders, grab the coins and find the exit.

## How to play

- **W A S D** = move
- Hold **Shift** = sprint
- Touch a ghost, bat, spider or spikes and you lose health. A potion heals you.

## Made with

Godot 4.7
